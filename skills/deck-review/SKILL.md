# Full review before delivery

[deck-review-001] While building, run lint with build-slides's `gslides.py check` for slides and build-docs's `docs.py check` for documents, and open the images of the changed pages yourself and fix them (`build-slides` "Reading the results", `build-docs` §3). This skill is the review of the whole material that runs after that, before returning it to the user. The steps and the files below are the same for every format; only the review prompt is split per format. A separate pair of eyes finds the cross-page defects that page-by-page checks miss (story, runs of the same pattern, uneven whitespace and density, the number of decorations) and the defects the maker overlooks (研究 1-3、AI 実践 1-4、中身 1-12; the abbreviations are in the table in `deck-workflow`). For fixes after delivery, how far to run (one round on the changed pages only, the whole material, or not at all) is decided by deck-flow-017 in `deck-workflow` → requests after delivery.

`/deck-review <Google Slides or Google Docs URL> [--talk]`. If the URL is omitted, use the URL written in the outline of the working folder. The URL decides the format (`/presentation/` is slides, `/document/` is a document). `--talk` is for materials that are spoken, such as LTs (slides only). Steps 1–6 below (check again, ask, verify findings, fix, rebuild and check) count as one round; run up to the round limit.

## Steps

```sh
K=<build-slides folder>/scripts   # the scripts/ folder of the skill build-slides (installed beside this skill's folder)
J=<build-docs folder>/scripts     # the scripts/ folder of the skill build-docs
C=<deck-review folder>/scripts    # this skill's copies of sheet.py and yomiyasu/ (the owner of sheet.py is build-slides)
python3 $K/gslides.py check <URL> --all [--talk] # Slides. Images of all pages (the checkpoint for cross-page review)
D=/tmp/slides_<first 8 chars of ID>              # Output of check (deck.json from presentations.get [not build/deck.json], deck.md, p-NN.webp)
python3 $J/docs.py check <URL>                    # Document (Google Docs)
D=/tmp/docs_<first 8 chars of ID>                # Output of check (doc.json, text.md, p-N.png)
uv run -q $C/sheet.py $D/sheet.webp $(ls $D/p-* | sort -V) --cols 4 --width 400 --per 16   # Contact sheet (16 pages each: sheet-1.webp …; sheet.webp if 16 pages or fewer)
```

1. **Check all pages again.** For slides, run `gslides.py check --all` and the contact sheet; for documents, run `docs.py check` and the contact sheet. If you just built the whole material and ran `check --all` (step 8 in `deck-workflow` → steps 6–8 for decks) and have not rebuilt since, reuse that output (`$D`) and contact sheet; do not run them again, and ask round 1 right away (step 3) without fixing first: put the remaining lint `要修正` items and what your whole-deck pass finds into the page JSON while its children look, and build them with round 1's fixes. Otherwise, if lint `要修正` items remain, fix them first.
2. **Confirm the audience** from the outline and write it into the review prompt ([ask.md](ask.md)). The outline's 「書き方の取り決め」 section is called 「書き方の決め」 in outlines written before 2026-11-01; treat either name as that section.
3. **Ask** the review children with the per-format prompt ([ask.md](ask.md)). In round 1, also start pass 2 of the source check.
4. **Verify the findings** one by one before fixing ([fix.md](fix.md)).
5. **Fix** what may be fixed and set aside what to ask ([fix.md](fix.md)); before fixing pages yourself, open what `desk.py recipes <material folder>` (`deck-workflow`; without it, the `grep` in "Workspace rules" of `desk-feedback` → routing) names that is not in your context yet. Copy the material's text before the first fix (deck-review-018 in [fix.md](fix.md)).
6. **Check the fixed pages again.** While putting in fixes, use `slides.py lint <URL>` (no build) to bring `要修正` to zero and `slides.py wording` to 「済み」 (deck-review-019 in [fix.md](fix.md); documents: `docs.py wording <URL>` after the round's build and check); after all of the round's fixes are in, build only once, run `gslides.py check <URL>` (takes images of the fixed pages only and writes `$D/changed.json`; for documents, `docs.py check <URL>` on all pages, because page breaks move), rebuild the contact sheet, compare the text before and after the fixes (deck-review-018 in [fix.md](fix.md)), and open the images of the fixed pages yourself (`$D/changed.json`). Then decide, by deck-review-006 below, whether to go back to step 3 with the fixed pages and the contact sheet ("From round 2" in [ask.md](ask.md)) or to deliver. Steps 1–6 count as one round; stop at the number of rounds in `[review] deck_review_max_rounds` in `desk.toml` (1–3; 3 if not set). If a fix increases defects, revert that fix, because the more rounds you run, the more likely a fix's side effects create new defects ("Basis" below).
   [deck-review-006] **How to end.** Check every fix before delivering. You check the fixed pages yourself in step 6 (their images, lint, the meaning-risk diff, and the contact sheet for drift across pages). Ask for the next round, with a new child, only for what that check cannot judge: pages added, deleted, split, or reordered, or content moved between pages (the story and the numbers across pages change, which images of single pages do not show); a heading, lead, or form that you chose yourself rather than the one the finding proposed (whether its point comes across is a first-time reader's call, deck-writing-001, not the maker's); or a defect on a fixed page that you could not settle. Everything else is settled on the images of the fixed pages, including a new form the finding proposed (the image shows whether it was drawn right), and then you end and deliver. Stop at the round limit regardless. Findings not fully fixed when you end (also when the count does not drop, and when the limit is 1 round) go in the table as 「残した（<number of rounds> ラウンドで打ち切り）」 when you deliver (so you do not end without checking after a fix, or deliver while hiding that the count did not drop). Each extra round costs a child's review (a median of 4.5–5.5 minutes) and the parent's fixes between rounds (a median of 12.8 minutes; 2026-11-17, the workspace's `Research/ai_desktools_timing_20261117.md`). In 6 sandbox runs of one 12-page deck under this rule (2 of them under a stricter one that also asked for a round after any change of layout; Claude Code, 2026-11-17), the review took 1–2 rounds instead of 2–3, the deck was delivered in a mean of 20.7 minutes against 29.7 in 4 runs under the earlier rule (the next round after any fix), and an independent reviewer found as many 「直す」 left in the delivered decks (a mean of 6.8 against 8.8; two passes per deck, which differed by up to 8 on the same deck). When round 2 was also dropped for headings and forms the maker chose (3 runs), 11.2 remained.
   [deck-review-013] **Before asking for the next round, write out and summarize the round.** Append the round's table ("Finding format" in [fix.md](fix.md)), the 「直したこと」 to add to the next round's prompt, and the questions for the user to `$D/review.md` (the same file as the table delivered in step 7). If you can call a context-summarizing tool, call it after that (deck-flow-005 and 015 in `deck-workflow`). In the continuation instructions, write which round you are about to ask for, the URL, `$D`, and the files to reread (`$D/review.md`, the outline, this file, [ask.md](ask.md), [fix.md](fix.md), `deck-workflow` → child agents, the review prompt, and [prompt_fix.md](prompt_fix.md) if you split the fixes). If you cannot call it, just append and ask for the next round. This step averages 68 calls per run, and 8 of 11 automatic summaries at 400k ran here (2026-10-14; the same record as deck-flow-005).
7. **Deliver.** After the last build and check, run the match with the saved material until it reports 「済み」 (deck-review-019). Return the Drive URL, the findings table ("Finding format" in [fix.md](fix.md)), and the list of questions for the user to the user. Write the delivered table (all rounds) to `$D/review.md` (do not rewrite the earlier rounds' rows appended in deck-review-013; add the last round's table. For findings carried over to the next round, write the page and 「何が起きているか」 with the previous round's wording; the ledger uses these two and the rule to identify the same finding), and import it into the rule ledger with `python3 <desk-feedback folder>/scripts/ledger.py review --deck <material folder> --table $D/review.md` (desk-dreaming counts results per rule. The same row is not counted twice, and the same row across rounds takes the later round's result). After checking, you may delete `$D`. For pages.py decks, also delete the `build/pages.py.bak*` left by `slides.py merge`.

Basis: the round limit and reverting when things get worse follow research on draw-look-fix loops (ACE: two thirds finish in one pass, limit of 3 passes, roll back when worse) (研究 1-3・1-4).
